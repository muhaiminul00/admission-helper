# User Guide

A walkthrough of Admission Helper from a student's point of view.

## Getting Started

1. Register an account at `/accounts/register/`, or ask an
   administrator to create one for you.
2. Log in. You'll land on the **Dashboard** - today's lectures/exams,
   overall progress, and a few "Study Advisor" insights.

## Navigation

The header has:

- The main links: **Dashboard**, **Routine**, **Exams**, **Progress**,
  **Revision**, **Analytics**.
- A **User Menu** (your username, top right) - **Profile** (your
  account details and a link to Notification Preferences), **Theme**
  (Light/Dark/System), and **Logout**.
- On a narrow screen, all of this collapses into a single hamburger
  menu.
- Staff users additionally get a **Staff** menu with **Datasets** and
  **Reminder Administration** - not visible to regular students.

## Tracking Progress

Every lecture topic has four independent activities, each worth 25%:

| Activity | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Class | Blue |
| Notes | Green |
| Revision | Amber |
| Practice | Purple |

Check them off from the **Lecture Detail** page (reached via the
Routine timeline or Analytics tables). You don't need to complete all
four before your progress shows - completing just "Class" already
shows 25%, not 0%.

Progress rolls up automatically: a Chapter's progress is the combined
progress of every topic in it; a Subject's is every chapter/lecture
under it; and the Dashboard's "Overall Completion" is everything,
system-wide. You never need to update a parent entity yourself.

The colored meter shown everywhere (Dashboard, Subject/Chapter
pages, Analytics tables) adapts automatically: small entities show
individual colored blocks, larger ones (many topics) show one
continuous bar - the meaning is identical either way.

## Revision

Once you fully complete a lecture (all four activities, on every
topic), it automatically gets three revision reminders scheduled (1
day, 3 days, and 7 days later). Track them at **Revision** - Due
Today / Overdue / Upcoming / Completed. Marking a revision complete is
one click from its detail page.

A lecture becomes **"Exam Ready"** once all three revision stages are
completed - shown as a badge everywhere that lecture is referenced.

## Exams

**Exams** shows a scrollable timeline centered on the nearest exam,
each with a readiness percentage and label (Ready / Good / Needs Work
/ High Risk) based on how much of its covered material you've
completed. Click through to an exam's detail page for the exact
subjects/chapters/lectures it covers and which ones you still need to
revise.

## Analytics

**Analytics** has five tabs: Insights (a "study advisor" - priority
chapters, neglected topics, exam-readiness scores), Subjects,
Chapters, Lectures (sortable/filterable completion tables), and Exam
Readiness.

## Routine Resources

**Routine Resources** shows the official routine PDF - the newest one
uploaded is always shown as "Latest Routine," with older ones
browsable underneath. Download button always matches whichever one is
currently displayed.

## Notification Preferences

At **Profile -> Notification Preferences**, turn each reminder type on
or off, set what time your Daily Study Agenda arrives, and set your
timezone (a searchable dropdown, not free text). Reminder types:

- **Daily Study Agenda** - today's lectures/revisions/exams, once a
  day at your chosen time.
- **Weekly Overview** - what's ahead, sent the first day of each new
  Routine Week.
- **Revision Due** - the moment a revision becomes due.
- **Weekly/Monthly Exam Reminders** - 1 day before (weekly exams);
  7/3/1 days before (monthly exams); and the morning of, either way.
- **Weekly Progress** - a summary of the week that just ended, sent
  alongside the next week's overview.

## Appearance

Use the **Theme** control in the User Menu to switch between Light,
Dark, and System (follows your OS setting automatically, live). This
is stored in your browser only - it isn't tied to your account and
doesn't sync across devices.
